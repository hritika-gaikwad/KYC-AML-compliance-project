It covers the full compliance lifecycle — from customer onboarding and risk classification to transaction monitoring, suspicious activity detection, and regulatory reporting — aligned with Indian and international AML regulations.

🔍 What's Inside
1. Customer KYC & Risk Scoring
Onboarded 75 customer profiles and built a weighted risk scoring model (0–100) using PEP status, occupation, income, source of funds, ID verification, and country risk. Classified customers into Low / Medium / High / Very High risk tiers per RBI KYC Master Directions 2016 and PMLA 2002.
2. Transaction Monitoring
Monitored 150 transactions across 8 types (Cash Deposit, RTGS, NEFT, Wire Transfer, IMPS, etc.) using rule-based AML logic to detect CTRs, STRs, velocity alerts, income mismatches, and cross-border red flags.
3. Suspicious Activity & SAR Filing
Flagged 26 suspicious transactions (17.3% alert rate) and filed 8 SARs/STRs to FIU-IND within the mandatory 72-hour window. Identified 11 cross-border transactions involving FATF grey-list jurisdictions (UAE, Singapore, Mauritius, BVI) and initiated Enhanced Due Diligence for 9 high-risk customers.
4. Compliance Dashboard
Built a live dashboard tracking 6 KPIs — Approval Rate, Alert Rate, SAR Rate, Risk Distribution, PEP Exposure, and CTR Rate — using 55+ cross-sheet Excel formulas including COUNTIF, VLOOKUP, SUMIFS, and Pivot Tables.
